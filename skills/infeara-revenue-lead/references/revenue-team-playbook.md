## 2. The funnel, stage by stage

```
AWARENESS   ── Brand, Content, Campaign, SEO ────────────▶ Visitors
             ↓ (Marketing Ops: tracking, scoring)
LEAD        ── Marketing Ops ────────────────────────────▶ Named contacts
             ↓ (score ≥ MQL threshold)
MQL         ── SDR (2-hour response SLA) ────────────────▶ Accepted lead
             ↓ (qualification framework)
SQL         ── SDR → Account Executive handoff ──────────▶ Meeting held
             ↓ (discovery, pain quantified)
OPPORTUNITY ── AE (+ Solutions Consultant) ──────────────▶ Validated solution
             ↓ (business case, mutual action plan)
PROPOSAL    ── Proposal Manager + AE ────────────────────▶ Submitted
             ↓ (negotiation, approvals)
WON         ── AE + Revenue Lead ────────────────────────▶ Contract
             ↓
EXPANSION   ── Partnerships + Account Manager ───────────▶ Renewal / growth
```

**Every arrow is a handoff with an SLA.** Broken handoffs are the single biggest revenue leak.

## 3. Weekly rhythm

| Day | Ceremony | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Revenue stand-up (30 min) | Revenue Lead | Week's target and blockers |
| Tue | Pipeline review (45 min) | Pipeline Orchestrator | Stage health + deal inspection |
| Wed | Marketing–sales handoff audit | Marketing Ops + Sales Ops | Rejection review, one fix each |
| Thu | Forecast submission | Sales Ops | Commit / best case |
| Fri | Campaign + content check | Campaign Manager + Content | Pacing and next week's assets |
| Monthly | Full funnel review | Pipeline Orchestrator | Conversion vs plan, bottleneck fix |
| Quarterly | Revenue reforecast | Revenue Lead | New target, coverage, capacity |

## 4. The SLA contract (both directions)

| Commitment | Owner | Target |
|---|---|---|
| First response to inbound | SDR | 2 business hours |
| Qualified meeting booked | SDR | 24 hours from MQL |
| Follow-up after demo | AE | 24 hours |
| Proposal delivered | Proposal Manager | 3 business days from scope sign-off |
| Rejection reason coded | AE | Same day |
| Lead-quality audit | Marketing Ops + Sales Ops | Weekly, 10 leads |
| Content request | Content Manager | 5 business days |
| Campaign tracking live | Marketing Ops | Before spend |

## 5. Arbitration rules (marketing vs sales)

1. **Dispute in the CRM**, with a reason code — never in a meeting.
2. **Monthly sample audit** of 20 rejected leads, jointly reviewed.
3. **Fix the definition, not the blame.** If a stage is ambiguous, rewrite it.
4. **Escalate on data**, not volume. Revenue Lead decides with funnel numbers.
5. **One number.** Marketing and sales report identical pipeline figures or the review stops until they do.

## 6. Diagnostics

| Symptom | Likely cause | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Traffic up, leads flat | Conversion or offer problem | SEO + Product Marketing |
| Leads up, MQLs flat | Targeting or scoring drift | Marketing Ops |
| MQLs up, SQLs flat | Qualification or SLA failure | SDR + Sales Ops |
| SQLs up, opps flat | Discovery discipline | AE |
| Opps up, wins flat | Value case, competition, pricing | Product Marketing + AE |
| Wins up, margin down | Discounting | Revenue Lead + CFO |
| Good wins, poor renewals | Handoff to delivery | Revenue Lead + Client Success |

## 8. What good looks like

- Marketing and sales quote the same pipeline number, every week.
- Every stage has an owner, an exit criterion, and an SLA.
- No campaign launches without verified tracking and a follow-up plan.
- No proposal is submitted without a compliance matrix and CFO-approved pricing.
- Every lost deal has a reason code and a monthly learning review.
- The team can explain, in funnel maths, exactly why the target will or will not be hit.
